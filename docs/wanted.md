# Wanted blueprints

Blueprints we would like to see, most of them for vertical software: the tools
one industry runs on. If you know one of these businesses from the inside,
[contribute a blueprint](../CONTRIBUTING.md#contribute-a-blueprint). If you
need one, add a thumbs-up to its request or
[open a request](https://github.com/segmentstream/build-your-own/issues/new?template=blueprint-request.yml).

## Vertical software

| Blueprint | For | Instead of | What makes it hard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Booking and scheduling | Salons, clinics, studios, consultants, rentals | Calendly, Acuity, Fresha | Availability across staff, rooms and equipment; no double booking under concurrency; deposits, no-shows and cancellations |
| Hotel and short-stay operations | Hotels, guest houses, serviced apartments | Cloudbeds, Mews | Room inventory by night, rate plans, channel availability, check-in and housekeeping states |
| Restaurant operations | Restaurants, cafés, dark kitchens | Toast, Square for Restaurants | Menus and modifiers, orders by station, stock depletion by recipe, shifts and tips |
| Field service | Trades, maintenance, installation, cleaning | ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro | Jobs, dispatch and routes, quotes to invoices, technicians on mobile, parts used on site |
| Construction projects | General contractors, builders | Procore, Buildertrend | Budgets and change orders, subcontractors, RFIs and submittals, retention |
| Property management | Landlords, property managers | AppFolio, Buildium | Units, leases, rent schedules and arrears, maintenance requests, owner statements |
| Legal practice | Law firms, legal teams | Clio | Matters, time and expenses, trust accounting, conflict checks, deadlines |
| Practice management | Therapy, physiotherapy, wellness | SimplePractice, Jane | Appointments, notes with strict access, packages and insurance; regulated data |
| Membership and classes | Gyms, studios, clubs | Mindbody, Glofox | Memberships and passes, class capacity and waitlists, recurring billing, freezes |
| Courses and cohorts | Training companies, schools | Teachable, Kajabi | Enrolments, cohorts, progress, certificates, payments and refunds |
| Recruiting | Any company that hires | Greenhouse, Lever, Workable | Openings, pipelines, structured interviews and scorecards, candidate privacy and retention |
| Nonprofit fundraising | Charities, foundations | Bloomerang | Donors and households, gifts and pledges, campaigns, gift aid and receipts |
| Insurance agency | Brokers and agencies | Applied Epic | Policies, renewals, carriers and commissions, claims follow-up |
| Real estate brokerage | Agents and brokerages | Follow Up Boss | Listings, buyers and sellers, showings, offers, commission splits |

## Operations and back office

| Blueprint | For | Instead of | What makes it hard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory, orders and purchasing (ERP-lite) | Product companies, wholesalers | NetSuite, Odoo, Cin7, inFlow | Stock by location and lot, reservations, purchase orders, landed cost, valuation that matches the ledger |
| Light manufacturing | Makers, food and drink producers | Katana, MRPeasy | Bills of materials, production orders, batch traceability, cost roll-up |
| Subscription billing | SaaS and subscription businesses | Chargebee, Recurly | Plans and price changes, proration, dunning, revenue recognition handed to bookkeeping |
| Spend and approvals | Finance teams | Spendesk, Ramp | Requests, approval chains by amount and budget, cards and receipts matched to bills |
| Support desk | Support teams | Intercom, Freshdesk | Shared inbox, SLAs, macros, AI-drafted replies with human approval, linked to customers |
| Projects and time | Agencies, consultancies | Asana, Harvest | Projects and retainers, time entries, budgets against actuals, invoicing time |
| HR and time off | Growing teams | HiBob, Personio | People records with strict access, contracts, leave balances by policy, approvals |
| Bank reconciliation | Finance teams | The reconciliation part of Xero and QuickBooks | Bank feeds, matching suggestions, transfers between accounts; extends bookkeeping |
| Data import kit | Anyone leaving a SaaS tool | Manual exports | Plan, dry run, apply and validate; resumable, idempotent loads; parity checks against the old system |

Country-specific modules (sales tax and VAT returns, payroll, e-invoicing) are
welcome as variations of the blueprint they extend, one country at a time.
